More data ≠ better data

  • But nobody can answer: how much of it is the same thing over and over?
  • Today's answer is LLM judges — slow, expensive, subjective.
  • So teams train on everything — or curate by gut feel.

Scale buys coverage, not variety.

What are the ~14 modes? (k-means sketch, k=14 — Vendi gives a count, not labels)

  • The top 5 modes alone cover 56% of all episodes
  • Every lab's episodes land in the same top modes — cross-lab redundancy, made visible
  • Two modes are mostly "long sessions" (rl2/eth, ~2,500 frames vs ~200–480) — recording style is an axis the score sees
  • Each mode's representative episode is in the README — paste it into Explore and watch it

New findings about EgoVerse (the dataset is weeks old — none of this exists elsewhere)

  • An "episode" is not the same object across labs: ~6.5 s median at mecka vs ~90–115 s at rl2/eth/song/wang — a 15× difference in what one training example is
  • Redundancy without duplicates: only 5% of episodes have a near-twin — the dataset isn't copy-pasted, it's thousands of independent recordings of the same few motions
  • Farthest-point sampling fails here: the standard diverse-subset heuristic scores no better than random in 741-dim (5.10 vs 5.11)

One build day on Modal

  • 12,542 episodes scored in 3.7 min — one .map(), one worker per episode, straight from R2
  • 92 containers at peak fan-out, ~25k function calls
  • 12,542 hover thumbnails pre-generated onto a Volume in ~8 min
  • Dashboard deployed as a web endpoint — 16 deploys: ~2 s cached, ~30–45 s with an image rebuild
  • Warm container answers in 0.3–0.5 s; rescore button spawns a fleet from inside the web app

The entire infrastructure is one Python file. No Docker, no YAML, no IAM.

Why it matters

  • Curation: keep the k episodes that maximize Vendi — train on less, lose nothing
  • Collection planning: the marginal curve says when to stop recording a task
  • Bring your own data: drop any features.npz on the dashboard

Honest limit: motion shape, not scene content. Visual embeddings on Modal GPUs are the designed next step.
